\paragraph{Zero-shot transfer on the development trio.}
Table~\ref{tab:main} is the paper's central result: the F-dev
\FdevDevMean\% headline from \S\ref{sec:intro}, trained on \FdevPicks M
picks from \FdevSets{} sets and evaluated on three sets it has never seen a
pick from. Broken out per set, DraftFM reaches \FdevBro\% / \FdevTmt\% /
\FdevSos\% on BRO / TMT / SOS against per-set ceilings of \CeilBro\% /
\CeilTmt\% / \CeilSos\%, recovering \RatioBro\% / \RatioTmt\%
/ \RatioSos\% of those ceilings (unaligned ratio; the pre-registered
normalized score on identical picks is \pending{aligned normalized scores,
dev trio}). Excluding forced picks lowers the three numbers to
\FdevBroNonForced\% / \FdevTmtNonForced\% / \FdevSosNonForced\%. The
deployment-mode skill bucket selected on dev is the \DeployBucket{}
win-rate bucket.

\paragraph{Reading the BRO row.}
BRO is the holdout on which \citet{bertram2024cards} report 55.44\%: the
strongest published unseen-set number, achieved with card features, learned
image embeddings, \emph{and} 16 usage statistics computed from human play
after the set's release. Our \FdevBro\% is below it. The comparison the
BRO row is designed for is the day-one setting, where usage statistics do
not exist: against the same paper's features-only representation
(33.6--35.6\%, single-set training) and every day-one-feasible baseline in
Table~\ref{tab:baselines}, multi-set training on public card features
closes most of the distance toward the usage-statistics-inclusive number
while using strictly release-day information. BRO is also the trio's
hardest set for transfer: its packs mix in a 63-card retro-artifact bonus
sheet. The gap between the BRO and TMT/SOS rows is analysed in
\S\ref{sec:analysis}.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
Table~\ref{tab:baselines}: random picking scores \BaseRandom\%, and an
hour-zero heuristic (prefer lower rarity for playables, follow the pool's
colors) reaches \BaseRarity\%: just below GPT-4o's 43\%
\citep{bertram2025urzagpt} and close to the expert-tuned 44.5\% of
\citet{ward2021drafting}, a useful caution about how much of drafting is
surface regularity. DraftFM's zero-shot numbers clear every
day-one-feasible row on all three dev sets. The three asterisked
post-release baselines bound what cheap human data buys within days of
release. Their MSH rows are filled in once the frozen evaluation runs.

\FdevSets-set universe (\FdevPicks M picks), under the fixed recipe.
Single-set training is the regime of \citet{bertram2024cards}'s
features-only anchor; the top rung is F-dev.
