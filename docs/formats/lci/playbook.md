# LCI play playbook

> Use this when: you are in a match and need a decision. Start with **How to act in Arena**, because the log hands you the legal moves for free.

Researched and fact-checked on 2026-09-10. Card facts live in [`cards.json`](cards.json) and [`combat-reference.md`](combat-reference.md), which are generated from data. This file records format judgement.

## Set primer

LCI is a board-centric TEMPO format that still reaches a real middle game. The median Quick Draft game ends on turn 9 (mode turn 8), but a quarter run past turn 10, and LCI is the 8th-fastest of 52 tracked Premier formats. Cheap evasive value creatures beat mid-range removal: the three best commons **by Premier GIH win rate** are all fliers (Oltec Cloud Guard, Miner's Guidewing, Waterwind Scout, all ~59% GIH), and only 12 of the common/uncommon creatures fly (verified: 12 of 104). *By the Quick-Draft GP-win-rate table in `lci-commons-review.md` the top common is instead Dead Weight {B}; the two files use different metrics from different queues and neither ranking is a measurement of the other — use GIH for card quality, GP for colour depth.* Ground boards gum up by turn 6-7 and are broken by fliers, crafted bodies, and the Caves that sacrifice for Discover 4. Blue is the best colour; Izzet, Azorius and Boros are the three best pairs in BOTH Premier and Quick Draft, and roughly half the Quick Draft field is in one of them — so default to expecting cheap artifacts, small fliers and white tap effects on the other side of the table, not big green creatures.

FIVE MECHANICS, EXECUTED CORRECTLY. EXPLORE: reveal the top card — a land goes to hand with NO counter; anything else puts a +1/+1 counter on the exploring permanent and you MAY bin the card. It resolves atomically (no responses mid-explore), it still happens if the creature has died, and repeated explores re-reveal the same card if you leave it on top. DISCOVER N: exile until a nonland of MV ≤ N, cast it free or take it to hand; it can never whiff into a land, the exiled cards are FACE UP for both players, X is forced to 0, and mandatory additional costs still apply. CRAFT: an activated ability that exiles the permanent as a cost — SORCERY SPEED ONLY, from the battlefield only. You can NOT craft in response to removal, killing the front face DOES end it, and the crafted back face is summoning-sick. DESCEND: permanent CARDS in your OWN graveyard only — tokens are not cards, instants and sorceries never count, sacrificed Caves do count, crafting (which exiles) does not. CAVES: 18 lands; the five common "Hidden" Caves enter tapped, tap for exactly ONE colour, and sac for {4}+that colour for Discover 4 at sorcery speed — they do NOT fix a splash.

WHAT DECKS WANT: two clean colours, 17 lands, 15-16 creatures, ~8 two-drops, at most two cards at MV6+. Three colours costs ~6.5 win-rate points in Quick Draft and even a splash costs ~3. Removal density does not decide archetype strength here — do not jam mediocre removal into a good UR or WU deck, and do not refuse good removal either.

BLOCKING IS UNUSUALLY SAFE AND UNUSUALLY DECISIVE. The entire set contains only 35 cards castable at instant speed (23 instants + 12 flash permanents; zero at mythic — verified on Scryfall, `e:lci (type:instant or keyword:flash)`), of which 8 are combat pump and 5 are flash creature bodies — so enumerating their open mana and colours usually settles a block with near-certainty. The numbers that matter: 3 damage kills 75% of LCI commons and 4 damage kills 86%, so FOUR TOUGHNESS is the format's safe line. Two open white mana threatens whichever creature is in combat, attacking OR blocking; THREE open white exiles a tapped creature. Two open black is Bitter Triumph; three is Join the Dead. Combat is simultaneous — only three cards in the set create a first-strike damage step. QUICK DRAFT SPECIFICS: the field is ~2 points softer than Premier, so low-variance lines convert; bots take removal 0.5-2 picks earlier than humans while passing tricks (Staggering Size, Acrobatic Leap, Family Reunion, Zoyowa's Justice) and Tithing Blade, so expect your own pool to be removal-light and expect OPPONENTS to be trick-rich and edict-rich. When a block is close, take the safe one.

## Deckbuilding targets

TWO CLEAN COLOURS, 40 cards. 17 lands / 23 spells, split roughly 15-16 creatures, ~4 instants+sorceries, ~3 noncreature artifacts, ~1 enchantment (this is the measured average LCI deck, reconstructed from 17lands play rates over 1.1M Premier games: 23.06 nonland + 17 land). CURVE: ~3 one-drops, ~8 two-drops, ~5 three-drops, ~3 four-drops, ~1-2 five-drops, at most TWO cards at MV6+. Cap the top end but do NOT cut a good four- or five-drop for a worse two-drop — GIH win rate by MV is flat from 2 to 5 (56.09-56.21%); only MV6 (-0.75pp) and MV7+ (-1.75pp) actually fall off. THREE COLOURS COSTS ~6.5 WIN-RATE POINTS in Quick Draft (57.9% two-colour vs 51.4% three-colour); even a splash costs ~3.1 points, and the Hidden Caves do NOT fix — each taps for exactly one colour. If DraftFM's build comes back three colours or with a splash, force a two-colour cut. LANDS: 16-17; shave to 16 only with cyclers/cantrips and a genuinely low curve. Play at most ONE OR TWO Hidden Caves (enter tapped, one colour, {4}+colour sac for Discover 4 at sorcery speed) — never on a turn you need to curve out. PRIORITIES: cheap evasive value over marginal removal (only 12 of 111 C/U creatures fly; the three best commons in the set are fliers — Oltec Cloud Guard 59.3%, Miner's Guidewing 59.2%, Waterwind Scout 59.1% GIH). Run one landcycler as free insurance (Soaring Sandwing / Seismic Monstrosaur / Nurturing Bristleback / Rampaging Spiketail / Marauding Brinefang, all cycle for {2}). NEVER-PLAY LIST (all sub-50% GIH, worse than a 17th land): Tectonic Hazard, Child of the Volcano, Kaslem's Stonetree, Acolyte of Aclazotz, Disturbed Slumber, Disruptor Wanderglyph, Vito's Inquisitor, Buried Treasure, Deep Goblin Skulltaker, Thousand Moons Infantry, Swashbuckler's Whip, Volatile Fault. Also cut the synergy traps: Hunter's Blowgun (50.1%), Waterlogged Hulk (50.9%), Frilled Cave-Wurm, Song of Stupefaction — even if the artifact count looks thin. DRAFT NOTE: bots take removal 0.5-2 picks EARLIER than humans (Abrade ALSA 2.07 QD vs 2.89 PD, Join the Dead 2.56 vs 3.65), so never pass removal expecting it to wheel; Staggering Size, Etali's Favor and Tithing Blade DO wheel — take the scarce card first. Gold signpost uncommons do NOT wheel, and only two are premium (Itzquinth 61.2%, Captain Storm 59.6%) — a high pick on Bartolomé, Nicanzil, Zoyowa or Uchbenbak is a sign the model is over-weighting gold.

## Mulligan rules

- Take the PLAY at the die roll. On-the-play win rate in LCI is 53.0% in Quick Draft — 7th-highest of the 52 tracked formats. Tempo beats the extra card in a 40-card deck here.
- KEEP any seven with 2-5 lands and castable spells. MULLIGAN 0, 1, 6 or 7 lands. That single line resolves most decisions.
- A mulligan is 'see a fresh seven, bottom one', not 'draw six'. Evaluate the best six of the seven you actually see; bottom your most expensive spell, not a land, unless bottoming a land still leaves you at 3+.
- One mulligan costs about **15** percentage points of win rate and two costs about **31** — measured on the 823,614-game 17lands LCI Premier Draft file (kept seven 57.2%, mulled to six 42.0%, to five 25.9%; see `limited-fundamentals.md` §11, which owns this table). So the bar for a marginal keep is low: keep almost anything functional, and almost never take a second mulligan on a hand that can function.
- Keep functional two-landers. Arena's Bo1 hand smoother generates two candidate opening hands and keeps the one whose land-to-spell ratio is closest to the deck's — so 0-land and 6-land sevens are rarer than raw probability, and a land-light hand you DO see is more likely a genuine outlier. (First-party, Arena tip `Queue_Tip_22`.) Smoothing affects the opening hand only, never your draws. **Whether it is reapplied after a mulligan is unverified — do not let it push a mulligan decision.**
- MULLIGAN any hand that cannot act before turn three. LCI is the 8th-fastest format tracked and 'a rough format to fall behind in'; an empty turn 2 and turn 3 is the cheapest way to lose.
- On the draw you may keep one turn slower and one land lighter — you are up a card and can afford the blocking-relevant two-drop over the proactive one. On the play you skip your first draw step, so slow and land-light hands are correspondingly worse.
- Bo1 with no sideboard: keep hands that function against a generic curve-out, never hands that are good only against a specific plan. There is no game two.
- Do not mulligan for speed. LCI is fast for a Limited format but it is not ONE-level aggro — the median game ends on turn 9 and a quarter run past turn 10, so a slow-but-functional hand is keepable.
- Colour-screwed keeps: a hand with both colours' lands and a two-drop beats a hand with more raw power and one colour. LCI is resource-rich (explore, Maps, Treasures, landcyclers), so assume the opponent hits every land drop — you must too.

## Turn loop

| step                                                 | decide                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1. PLAN DURING THEIR TURN                            | The rope is the last 30 seconds and it is enough to execute a plan, not form one. Before your untap, know: your land drop, your intended casts and sequence, your attack, and the block you would make on the crack-back. The active-player timer starts at 61s and GROWS with each action, so acting early buys clock — never sit computing the whole turn first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2. UPKEEP / DRAW — re-read the board                 | Record their untapped lands by colour, their hand size, their graveyard PERMANENT count, their artifacts and uncrewed Vehicles, and the +1/+1 counters actually on their creatures (explore and craft mean printed P/T is routinely wrong).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 3. LAND DROP, FIRST, EVERY TURN                      | It is a special action — it cannot be countered or responded to, so there is never a reason to hold it. Play the untapped land you need this turn; a Hidden Cave only on a turn you do not need to curve out. A permanent that TRANSFORMS into a land does not use your land drop — do both.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 4. PRECOMBAT MAIN — take all sorcery-speed value NOW | Crack Maps (sorcery speed — they cannot save a creature in combat), activate Craft, use Sunshot Militia / Adaptive Gemguard / Captivating Cave, sacrifice a Hidden Cave for Discover 4. Crack a Map precombat only if the +1/+1 counter changes the attack; otherwise second main. Remember tapping your own creatures for these costs you blockers on the crack-back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 5. DECIDE: BEATDOWN OR CONTROL — once, then commit   | Count both clocks: your power vs their life, their power vs your life. If you have the flier and they do not, stop trading on the ground and defend behind one evasive clock. If they have it, trade the ground down now and race, or find removal. Re-check only when the board materially changes. Stop half-attacking.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 6. ATTACK CHECK — three questions before declaring   | (a) If everything I attack with taps, what does their UNTAPPED board do to me next turn? (b) Read their open mana against the trick table — 2 white = Cosmium Blast/Sawblades, 3 white = Quicksand Whirlpool EXILE, 1 blue = Cogwork Wrestler, 2 black = Bitter Triumph, 3 black = Join the Dead, 2 red = Abrade or first strike, 2 green = +3/+3 trample or a one-sided Huatli's Final Strike, 6 of anything = Runaway Boulder. (c) Do they have an uncrewed Vehicle plus an untapped 1-power creature? Do not attack your best creature into two untapped white sources unless you can afford to lose it or the attack wins the game. |
| 7. DEPLOY AROUND SWEEPERS                            | Against white at five mana, keep a power-2-or-less body back (Unstable Glyphbridge). Against black at three mana, do not over-extend a board of X/1s and X/2s (Malicious Eclipse). Against red, a board of 1/1 tokens dies to Tectonic Hazard for {R}. Against black at two mana, NEVER be down to exactly one good creature (Tithing Blade edict).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 8. POSTCOMBAT MAIN — deploy what has no combat use   | Creatures cast after combat are still full blockers on their turn (summoning sickness does not stop blocking), so the opponent faces a fresh untapped body. Cast the creature that was not going to attack here, not precombat, unless casting it precombat baits or enables an attack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 9. TURN 8 ONWARD — never pass with unspent mana      | A stalled LCI board is won by whoever converts spare mana into board every turn: sac a Hidden Cave for Discover 4 ({4}+colour), sac Captivating Cave for two counters, make a Gnome with Envoy of Okinec Ahau, tick Adaptive Gemguard, craft. All sorcery-speed — sequence them in your own main phase. If you cannot win the mana-sink war, break the stall NOW.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 10. HOLDING UP INSTANTS                              | You get automatic stops on their begin combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, first-strike damage and END STEP. You get nothing in either upkeep, either draw step, or their main phases — click the phase on either player's phase bar to add a stop if you need one. Cast damage tricks in the declare-blockers window; cast anything that changes blocking LEGALITY at declare attackers or earlier.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 11. END THE TURN                                     | Shift+Enter (Pass Turn) — it still stops for your blocks. Never press Enter twice: the second press CANCELS the pass. If holding an instant for their end step, the default stop already covers it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 12. TRADE EARLY, HOARD LATE                          | Turns 1-4: take any even trade to stay on parity; play a creature every turn. Turns 4-5: the swing window — take the profitable trade or use the removal now. Turn 7+: stop trading and look for the evasive or oversized threat; trades only help whoever holds the stall-breaker, so check first whether that is you. Spend life early, hoard it late.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Blocking procedure

Canonical procedure: [`../../rules/blocking-procedure.md`](../../rules/blocking-procedure.md).

| step                                                               | decide                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0. PRE-COMPUTE during their turn, before attackers are declared    | The rope is 30 seconds and it is not enough to think from scratch. While they untap and draw, record: (a) their untapped lands BY COLOUR, (b) cards in their hand, (c) count of PERMANENT cards in THEIR OWN graveyard (artifact/creature/enchantment/land/planeswalker only — never instants, sorceries or tokens), (d) their artifacts and any uncrewed Vehicles, (e) my life total and their total attacking power. Have the block picked before the DeclareBlockersReq arrives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 1. LEGALITY FILTER — what may block what                           | Flying can only be blocked by flying/reach. LCI has FOUR reach creatures in the whole set (Poison Dart Frog, Mineshaft Spider, Panicked Altisaur, Colossadactyl) — if I have none, their flier is unblockable, full stop. Menace REQUIRES two or more blockers (Deep Goblin Skulltaker, Dinotomaton, Uchbenbak). Rampaging Ceratops needs three. Tapped creatures cannot block. SUMMONING-SICK CREATURES CAN BLOCK — a creature I cast this turn is a full blocker; a creature THEY cast this turn was a full blocker too. Also check forced blocks: Disturbed Slumber's 4/4 land 'must be blocked if able' and Arena maximises requirements, so it can drag my intended blocker off the real attacker.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2. LETHAL CHECK — is blocking mandatory?                           | Sum unblocked damage against my life. If taking it all kills me, blocking is not optional: chump the biggest non-trampler first (see step 6). Then compute THEIR life vs my board: if I win the race by attacking next turn, a block that costs me an attacker is a real loss — count both clocks before valuing any creature.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 3. TRICK READ — price the attack by their open mana and colour     | Add the worst plausible trick to each attacker, weighted by how often it is actually played in LCI Quick Draft. ## Rules traps

- DAMAGE ASSIGNMENT ORDER NO LONGER EXISTS (removed with Foundations, Nov 2024). When you multi-block, the ATTACKER divides its damage freely among your blockers with no lethal-first rule and no priority window — they pick which of your creatures dies. Any plan built on 'they must assign 3 to my 3/3 first' loses creatures you thought were safe.
- SUMMONING-SICK CREATURES CAN BLOCK. CR 509.1a requires only that a blocker be untapped. The creature they just cast is a full-value blocker; so is yours. Modelling blocking with the ATTACKING rules is the single most likely combat bug.
- CRAFT IS SORCERY-SPEED ONLY and only from the BATTLEFIELD (CR 702.167a). You cannot craft in response to removal — attempting it burns rope and loses the permanent. Craft pre-emptively in your own main phase. Killing a craft artifact DOES end the threat; it does not craft back from the graveyard (the graveyard only supplies MATERIALS). And the crafted back face is a NEW object with summoning sickness — it blocks the turn it arrives but cannot attack, so never plan lethal on the turn you craft.
- TRAMPLE REVERSES THE CHUMP RULE. Against a non-trampler, a blocked creature whose blockers are all gone deals NO damage (509.1h/510.1c) — chumping locks the damage out and their removal is wasted. Against a trampler (702.19d), the FULL power goes to your face. Do the arithmetic before deciding a chump 'saves' you.
- Tapping a creature after attackers or blockers are declared does NOTHING (CR 506.4b) — it stays in combat and still deals damage. Tap effects (Lodestone Needle, Thousand Moons Crackshot) must resolve at or before the declare-attackers step. Likewise, granting flying/menace after blockers are declared is wasted mana (509.1b).
- MAPS ARE SORCERY-SPEED ({1}, {T}, Sacrifice: target creature you control explores). They cannot grow a blocker or save an attacker mid-combat, and NO LCI card causes an explore at instant speed — explore is never a combat surprise. But a Map token is an artifact, not a creature, so it CAN be cracked the turn it is created: crack precombat if the counter changes an attack.
- DESCEND COUNTS ONLY PERMANENT CARDS IN THE CASTER'S OWN GRAVEYARD — artifact, creature, enchantment, land, planeswalker, battle. Instants and sorceries never count. TOKENS ARE NOT CARDS, so sacrificing a Map or Treasure advances nothing. Sacrificed Caves DO count (a land card hits the graveyard). Crafting EXILES and adds nothing.
- EXPLORE resolves atomically — no player can respond mid-explore. Land goes to hand and NO counter is placed; a nonland gives a +1/+1 counter and you MAY bin it. If they kill the explorer with the trigger on the stack, take the card selection anyway. Repeated explores (Over the Edge, Defossilize) re-reveal the SAME card if you leave it on top — bin the first nonland if you actually want to dig.
- VEHICLES ARE LIVE BLOCKERS. Crew has no timing restriction, so an 'inert' Vehicle plus any untapped 1-power creature is a blocker they can produce after you attack. But crewing TAPS their creatures, which turns on your Cosmium Blast, Spring-Loaded Sawblades and the {2}{W} Quicksand Whirlpool.
- PETRIFY does not stop triggers, statics, ETBs, or CREWING (crew is the Vehicle's activated ability, CR 702.122a — so summoning-sick and Petrified creatures can both crew). It does shut off craft. Never spend a second removal spell on a Petrified creature.
- DISCOVER never whiffs into a land; the exiled cards are FACE UP, so read them for free information about their deck and expect them to read yours. A card cast off discover is cast during resolution, so an instant-speed discover can deploy a sorcery on your turn — but X is forced to 0 and MANDATORY additional costs still apply (a discovered Bitter Triumph still costs a discard or 3 life).
- Damage is MARKED and is removed at cleanup every turn. A 4/4 that took 3 last turn is a fresh 4/4. Within one turn damage stacks, and it counts toward trample's lethal calculation.
- WARD, HEXPROOF AND SHROUD DO NOTHING IN COMBAT. Ward is a targeting tax only. Marauding Brinefang's ward {3} does not save it from a chump block. And Tithing Blade is an EDICT, so ward/hexproof/protection are all irrelevant against it.
- LCI IS ESSENTIALLY FIRST-STRIKE-FREE: only Bonehoard Dracosaur (mythic), Breeches (rare) and Kinjalli's Dawnrunner (uncommon, double strike) create an extra damage step, plus Ancestors' Aid at {1}{R}. If red is not open, treat all combat as simultaneous. When first strike IS in combat, you get a real priority window between the two damage steps.
- A red opponent with {2}{R} and cards in hand can DISCARD Trumpeting Carnosaur to deal 3 to a creature without ever casting a spell — the format's most common 'where did that come from'. Treat 3-toughness creatures as at risk even when their hand looks empty of removal.
- Against a WHITE opponent untapping with FIVE mana, do not commit three or more real creatures: Unstable Glyphbridge destroys all creatures except one power-2-or-less creature per player. Keep a small body back as insurance — they cannot take that one from you. Get Lost ({1}{W} rare instant) also means white is not safe outside combat.
- KILLING DEEP-CAVERN BAT RETURNS the exiled card to their hand. If it took their bomb, leave the Bat alive. Killing an artifact enchanted by Zoetic Glyph still gives them discover 3 when the Aura falls off — bounce or exile the artifact instead.
- Pugnacious Hammerskull (rare 6/6 for three) stun-counters itself when it attacks with no other Dinosaur, so it stays TAPPED through your following turn — the turn after it swings, your attack is free. Thousand Moons Infantry (2/4) untaps during EACH other player's untap step, so it attacks and still blocks. Recount blockers after every trigger.
- Bedrock Tortoise makes their toughness>power creatures deal damage equal to TOUGHNESS (their 1/4 hits for 4) and grants their team hexproof on their own turn. Recompute every block against it.
- Mana pools empty at the end of every STEP and phase. Floating mana in your precombat main is gone by declare attackers. Relic's Roar ({U}) can target YOUR creature to SHRINK it to base 4/3 — it sets base P/T, so it turns your 6/6 into a 4/3 mid-combat (counters still apply on top).
- Decking is never a plan and never a fear: only 0.34% of games reach turn 20+. But past turn 14 with a small library, DECLINE the optional 'you may mill two' triggers.
- Do not quote 17lands GIH win rates for LCI Quick Draft as fact — the public endpoint publishes GIH for only about four QD cards. All card rankings in this playbook are Premier-derived; PLAY RATE and ALSA are the reliable Quick-Draft-specific signals. Never compare a raw QD win rate to a Premier one: the QD baseline is ~2pp higher purely because the field is weaker.
